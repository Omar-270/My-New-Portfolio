01 · ABOUT

A journey from Arduino to data.

I'm Omar Ahmed Osman, a high school student with a strong curiosity for technology and continuous learning.

My journey started with Arduino, but as I explored different areas of technology, I discovered that Data Analysis was the field I enjoyed most.

Through the Digital Egypt Cubs Initiative (DECI), I developed my skills in Python, Data Analysis, and other technical areas. The experience also helped me grow personally, becoming more open to people and improving my communication and presentation skills.

What I enjoy most about Data Analysis is turning raw and seemingly meaningless data into valuable insights that can help businesses understand their customers and make better decisions. I'm currently focusing on strengthening my Data Analysis skills, especially SQL, while working toward building a career in this field.
